Omar El Bachyr

PhD Researcher · University of Luxembourg

TruX Research Group

SnT, University of Luxembourg

I work in the TruX Research Group, supervised by Prof. Jacques Klein, with guidance from Prof. Tegawendé F. Bissyandé and Prof. Saad Ezzini, in collaboration with BGL BNP Paribas.

My research focuses on information retrieval (IR), multimodal document understanding, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over visually rich documents.

I am also interested in multilingual and low-resource retrieval and RAG, particularly for Luxembourgish.

Research Interests

  • Information Retrieval (IR) & Multimodal Retrieval
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
  • Multilingual & Low-Resource Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Selected Publications

  1. SIGIR-AP 2026
    VisR-RAG-Fin: Benchmarking Multimodal RAG over Visually Rich Financial Documents
    Omar El Bachyr, Laura Maria Bernardy, Saad Ezzini, Jacques Klein, and 5 more authors
    In 4th International ACM SIGIR Conference on Information Retrieval in the Asia Pacific (SIGIR-AP), Main Research Track, 2026
    Abstract arXiv HTML Code

    VisR-RAG-Fin introduces an open-source benchmark for page-level question answering over visually rich financial documents, preserving their layout and visual content. An empirical study compares text-only, multimodal, and hybrid retrieval and generation configurations to guide the design and evaluation of multimodal RAG systems.

  2. APSEC 2026
    RAGit: Turning Document Preprocessing into an Experimental Variable for RAG
    Omar El Bachyr, Saad Ezzini, Tegawendé F. Bissyandé, and Jacques Klein
    In 33rd Asia-Pacific Software Engineering Conference (APSEC), Tool Demonstration Track, 2026
    Abstract arXiv Video Code

    RAGit is an open-source workbench for reproducible evaluation of document preprocessing through downstream retrieval. It supports configurable parsing and chunking, reusable experiment artifacts, and dense and late-interaction retrieval, mapping different retrieval units to a common page-level ranking for controlled comparisons.

  3. LaTeLL 2026
    LëtzCross: A Cross-Lingual Page-Level Benchmark for Multimodal Retrieval over Luxembourgish Documents
    Omar El Bachyr, Fred Philippy, Laura Maria Bernardy, Saad Ezzini, and 2 more authors
    In International Conference on Language Technologies for Low-resource Languages (LaTeLL), Sep 2026
    Abstract DOI arXiv Code

    LëtzCross benchmarks cross-lingual page-level retrieval over Luxembourgish PDFs using queries in four languages. Page-image retrievers outperform OCR-based text-only retrievers in the system-level comparison. Fine-tuning transfers across languages, with multilingual training that includes Luxembourgish producing the strongest results on Luxembourgish queries.

  4. ICSE-SEIP 2026
    Empirical Evaluation of PDF Parsing and Chunking for Financial Question Answering with RAG
    Omar El Bachyr, Yewei Song, Saad Ezzini, Jacques Klein, and 4 more authors
    In Proceedings of the IEEE/ACM 48th International Conference on Software Engineering: Software Engineering in Practice, 2026
    Abstract DOI arXiv Code

    This study evaluates how PDF parsing, chunking, and overlap affect RAG-based question answering on two financial benchmarks, including the newly introduced TableQuest. The results provide practical guidance on preserving document structure and improving answer correctness in PDF-processing pipelines.

  5. ISCRAM 2024
    M-CATNAT: A Multimodal dataset to analyze French tweets during natural disasters
    Badreddine Farah, Omar El Bachyr, Guillaume Cleuziou, Anaïs Halftermeyer, and 4 more authors
    In Proceedings of the International ISCRAM Conference, May 2024
    Abstract DOI

    M-CATNAT presents a French multimodal dataset annotation effort for natural-disaster tweets, following CrisisMMD guidelines. Separate annotations for text, images, and their combination address gaps in multilingual and multimodal crisis resources, with a target of 1,430 tweets and over 4,500 labels.
