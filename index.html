Omar El Bachyr

PhD Researcher · University of Luxembourg

TruX Research Group

SnT, University of Luxembourg

I work in the TruX Research Group, supervised by Prof. Jacques Klein, with guidance from Prof. Tegawendé F. Bissyandé and Prof. Saad Ezzini, in collaboration with BGL BNP Paribas.

My research focuses on information retrieval (IR), multimodal document understanding, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over visually rich documents.

I am also interested in multilingual and low-resource retrieval and RAG, particularly for Luxembourgish.

Research Interests

  • Information Retrieval (IR) & Multimodal Retrieval
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
  • Multilingual & Low-Resource Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Selected Publications

  1. APSEC 2026
    RAGit: Turning Document Preprocessing into an Experimental Variable for RAG
    Omar El Bachyr, Saad Ezzini, Tegawendé F. Bissyandé, and Jacques Klein
    In 33rd Asia-Pacific Software Engineering Conference (APSEC), Tool Demonstration Track, 2026
    Abstract arXiv Video Code

    RAGit is an open-source workbench for comparing document preprocessing choices in retrieval-augmented generation. Configurable parsing, chunking, indexing, retrieval, and evaluation stages support controlled experiments, reusable artifacts, and a shared page-level evaluation across different retrieval units.

  2. LaTeLL 2026
    LëtzCross: A Cross-Lingual Page-Level Benchmark for Multimodal Retrieval over Luxembourgish Documents
    Omar El Bachyr, Fred Philippy, Laura Maria Bernardy, Saad Ezzini, Jacques Klein, and Tegawendé F. Bissyandé
    In International Conference on Language Technologies for Low-resource Languages (LaTeLL), Sep 2026
    Abstract DOI arXiv Code

    LëtzCross evaluates cross-lingual retrieval over Luxembourgish PDF pages using queries in English, French, German, and Luxembourgish. The benchmark combines textual and visual questions to compare text-only and page-image retrievers, and investigates how single-language and multilingual fine-tuning transfer across query languages.

  3. ICSE-SEIP 2026
    Empirical Evaluation of PDF Parsing and Chunking for Financial Question Answering with RAG
    Omar El Bachyr, Yewei Song, Saad Ezzini, Jacques Klein, Tegawendé F. Bissyandé, Anas Zilali, Ulrick Ble, and Anne Goujon
    In Proceedings of the IEEE/ACM 48th International Conference on Software Engineering: Software Engineering in Practice, 2026
    Abstract DOI arXiv Code

    This study examines how PDF parsing and chunking choices affect retrieval-augmented question answering over financial documents. Experiments on two benchmarks, including the newly introduced TableQuest dataset, compare parsers, chunking strategies, and overlap settings to guide the design of document-processing pipelines.

  4. ISCRAM 2024
    M-CATNAT: A Multimodal dataset to analyze French tweets during natural disasters
    Badreddine Farah, Omar El Bachyr, Guillaume Cleuziou, Anaïs Halftermeyer, Cécile Gracianne, Samuel Auclair, Adel Hafiane, and Raphaël Canals
    In Proceedings of the International ISCRAM Conference, May 2024
    Abstract DOI

    M-CATNAT introduces a French-language dataset for multimodal analysis of natural-disaster tweets. Its annotation approach treats tweet text, images, and their combination separately, extending crisis-management resources beyond English and supporting automated analysis of social media during disasters.
