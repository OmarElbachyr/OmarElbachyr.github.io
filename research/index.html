Research

I study information retrieval, multimodal document understanding, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), with an emphasis on evaluation and low-resource languages.

Research Interests
Information Retrieval (IR) & Multimodal Retrieval

Retrieval from documents combining text, tables, charts, and visual information.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Retrieval quality, document question answering, and reliable generation.

Multilingual & Low-Resource Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Multilingual and cross-lingual retrieval, particularly for Luxembourgish and other low-resource languages.

Research Projects & Collaborations
Document Retrieval & Question Answering

SnT × BGL BNP Paribas

Doctoral research on retrieval and RAG over complex, visually rich documents, including tables and diverse layouts. The work includes developing benchmarks and evaluation methods for retrieval and question answering. Financial documents serve as a use case within the research collaboration with BGL BNP Paribas.

Luxembourgish Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation

SnT · Collaborative research initiative

Collaborative research on multimodal RAG for Luxembourgish. My contribution focuses on developing synthetic training data and evaluation benchmarks, and investigating retrieval training strategies.

Cross-Lingual & Low-Resource Retrieval

Broader research interests

Cross-lingual retrieval, multilingual representations, and RAG evaluation for languages with limited resources.
